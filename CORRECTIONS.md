[Source](https://welingkar.org/fmb/post-graduate-program-in-entrepreneurship-management/)

Eligibility: kisi bhi stream me graduation, 50% marks ke saath. Fee **₹4 lakh + GST**, EMI available, Batch 2026. [Source](https://www.welingkar.org/mediaandentertainment/)
