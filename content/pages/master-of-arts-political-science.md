### Frequently Asked Questions

###### Questions Asked by Students

## Have Any Questions?

**1. What is the eligibility for Sikkim Manipal University’s online MA?**

Candidates must have completed 10+2+3-year bachelor’s degree from a recognized university/institution, or an equivalent qualification as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities.

**2. What is the application process for online degrees at Sikkim Manipal University?**

The application process is completely online and applications can be submitted at www.onlinemanipal.com by providing the required details. If you need further assistance, then you can reach out to our admission counsellors at +91-7996 799 900 and they will assist you with the application process.

**3. What are the documents required for admission into Sikkim Manipal University’s online degree programs?**

Mandatory documents:
- Proof of identity – Aadhaar card (mandatory for Indian citizens)
- Proof of address – Aadhaar card, passport, voter’s ID, gas bill, bank statement, post-paid mobile statement
- Class 10 & 12 marksheet.
- Graduation marksheet (for PG students)
Non-mandatory documents:
- Graduation degree certificate for PG students
- Scholarship documents
- Defense Service certificate
- Divyaang certificate
- Reserved Category certificate
- Government ID card

**4. *What are the terms & conditions for EMIs and other easy financing options?**

*Zero cost EMI option is available only on payment of annual fee.
The EMI amount mentioned above applies to the annual fee and must be paid monthly for 12 months.
Approval of loans from our loan partners depends on applicants meeting credit norms and submitting required documents. Loan partners may impose other fees and may have additional terms & conditions that must be met by applicants.
For further details on EMIs and other easy financing options, please contact our admission counsellors.

**5. What is the application fee for Sikkim Manipal University’s online programs?**

The application fee for Sikkim Manipal University’s online programs is INR 500 (inclusive of GST). This is a separate fee which is over and above the course fee. Please note that the application fee is non-refundable.
