### Admission Criteria

Indian Students:
Educational qualification
Candidates must have completed 10+2+3-year bachelor’s degree from a recognized university/institution or an equivalent qualification as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities.

NRI & Foreign students:
Educational qualification
Candidates must have completed 10+2+3-year bachelor’s degree from a recognized university/institution or an equivalent qualification as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities.
Candidates who have completed 10+2 education or graduate level education outside India must produce a certificate of equivalence issued by the Association of Indian Universities.
Other requirements
Candidates who are not Indian citizens or residing outside India and holding NRE or PIO status will be billed an international fee for respective degrees & may need to submit documents mentioned but not limited to CV, address proof, valid visa, PR card & passport copy as part of the application process.

**1. What is the eligibility for online MA in Economics?**

Indian students

Educational qualification
Candidates must have a 10+2+3-year bachelor’s degree from a recognized university/institution, or an equivalent qualification as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities
International students
Educational qualification
Candidates must have a 10+2+3-year bachelor’s degree from a recognized university/institution, or an equivalent qualification as recognized by the Association of Indian Universities
Candidates who have completed their 10+2 or graduate level education outside India must produce a certificate of equivalence issued by the Association of Indian Universities
Other requirements
Candidates who are not Indian citizens, or residing outside India, and holding NRE or PIO status will be billed an international fee for respective degrees & may need to submit documents mentioned but not limited to CV, address proof, valid visa, PR card & passport copy as part of the application process.

**2. Who can apply to Manipal University Jaipur’s online MA in Economics?**

- Students with a bachelor’s degree in economics or a related field
- Fresh graduates who wish to launch a career in the field of economics
- Working professionals who want to gain deeper knowledge in economics

**3.Are international students eligible for Manipal University Jaipur’s online degree programs?**

Yes, NRIs and foreign nationals can pursue Manipal University Jaipur’s online degree programs.

**4. What is the application process for online degrees at Manipal University Jaipur?**

The application process is completely online, and applications can be submitted at www.onlinemanipal.com by providing the required details. Candidates can also apply via this link.
If you need further assistance, then you can reach out to our admission counsellor at +91-7304-000-444 and they will assist you with the application process.

**5.What is the application fee for Manipal University Jaipur’s online programs?**

The application fee for Manipal University Jaipur’s online programs is INR 500 (inclusive of GST). Please note that the application fee is non-refundable.
